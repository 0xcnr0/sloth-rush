One horizontal lane of a vintage tin-lithograph toy racetrack, the printed tin sheet seen straight on at eye level, very wide short letterbox, empty, no perspective.

The lane printed in the style of a toy box lid: dark navy field, a cream track strip through the middle, a thin gold pinstripe frame, small printed radiating speed lines along the top edge.

Printed with only three or four ink colours the way tin lithography was, colour plates very slightly out of register against the black line work, halftone dot screens standing in for shading, flat matte litho ink on a faint metal sheen.

No characters, robots, toys, cars, animals, hands, faces, text, letters, numbers or logos.
