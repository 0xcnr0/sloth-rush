A 2D game sprite sheet: the separate body parts of a vintage lithographed tin
wind-up toy robot, laid out apart with clear gaps, none overlapping.

Every part is drawn completely flat and straight-on, no perspective, no
isometric angle — as if cut from one flat illustration of the robot.

The parts: a square block head with two big round friendly eyes and a curved
smile on its front, a wide rectangular torso with a front chest panel of
printed dials and gears, two thick block arms each ending in a rounded red
hand, two thick block legs each ending in a red block foot, and a gold brass
butterfly winding key.

Flat printed colour, thick dark outline, rivets on the seams. Cobalt blue
torso, arms and legs, silver-grey head, red hands and feet. Plain cream
background, no shadow.
