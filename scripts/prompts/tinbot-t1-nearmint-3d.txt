A chunky wind-up tin toy robot, boxy build with softly rounded edges: square
block head, wide rectangular torso, thick straight block arms and legs of
uniform width, rounded block hands, small rounded feet.

On the front of the head, two big round eyes and a small curved smile. The
top of the head is one smooth bare uninterrupted surface.

On the centre of the back panel, a large winding key: a flat oval ring handle
standing perpendicular to the back on a short square shaft, the ring about a
third the width of the torso.

The chest is a single smooth raised rectangular panel, plain and empty.

Standing in a T-pose, arms straight out to the sides at shoulder height, legs
straight and parallel. Chunky toy proportions, head about a third of total
height. Simple closed solid geometry.
