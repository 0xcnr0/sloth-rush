A vintage lithographed tin wind-up toy robot, full body, T-pose with arms
straight out to the sides.

Boxy chunky build, softly rounded edges: square block head, wide rectangular
torso, thick block arms ending in rounded block hands, thick block legs the
same width as the arms, small red block feet. Stocky and solid — the limbs
are chunky blocks, never thin rods.

Big round eyes and a curved smile printed on the head. The top of the head is
smooth and bare — no antenna. The torso sides are smooth, flat and bare.

Flat printed colour with crisp black outlines. Cobalt blue torso, arms and
legs, silver-grey head, red hands and feet. The chest panel carries printed
dials and gears in red and yellow. Rivets along the seams. Glossy lacquer,
factory-fresh. Plain cream background.
