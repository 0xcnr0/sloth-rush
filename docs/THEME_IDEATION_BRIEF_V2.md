# Theme Ideation Brief v2 — for an external AI tool

**Neden v2:** v1'deki brief teknik/mekanik olarak doğruydu (4 aracın hepsi rarity/evrim ayrımını doğru anladı) ama sonda verdiğim iki talimat ("cute mascot'tan kaçının", "gerçek bir stretch fikir de ekleyin") tam da istenmeyen yöne itti — dört araç da soyut/sanatsal kavramlara kaydı (kozmik varlıklar, origami ruhlar, maske-kaplar). Bu sürüm o iki talimatı kaldırıyor, somut/gündelik nesne temelli fikir istiyor, ve her öneriden gerçek oyun referansı ile "buna benziyor" demesini zorunlu kılıyor.

**Kullanım:** Aynı araçlara (ChatGPT, Gemini, Ludo.ai, Grok) bu yeni metni gönder.

---

## Prompt

```
# Creative Brief v2: Theme Ideation for a Racing Game (keep it simple)

I'm building a mobile-first competitive racing game. I asked a
similar question before and got very abstract, art-installation-style
answers (living comets, origami spirits, mask-vessels). That's not
what I want. I want simple, concrete, immediately-lovable ideas —
think "toy on a shelf," not "gallery piece."

## What actually works in this genre (read this before answering)

The best examples in this genre take something mundane, small, or
overlooked — an everyday object, a small appliance, a tiny creature,
a simple mechanical device — and make it charming and heroic purely
through simple, rounded, chunky design plus a bit of "racing gear"
personality (paint job, decals, a tiny helmet, a spoiler, exhaust
smoke, googly eyes, whatever fits). I want ideas I can picture
immediately in one sentence, not abstract mood-board language.

Keep the visual language simple: rounded shapes, bold clean outlines,
bright saturated colors, a small number of parts per character.

## What the game actually is

- Genre: free-to-play competitive racing game with blockchain-based
  collectible ownership (NFTs on an Ethereum Layer 2 called Base)
- Core loop: player claims a free starter racer at no cost (gasless
  mint) → races it to earn in-game currency → can optionally pay a
  small real-money fee to permanently upgrade (the starter is
  destroyed/"burned" and a new, upgraded racer is minted in its
  place) → trains and customizes the upgraded racer → competes in
  higher-stakes race formats
- 4 racers compete per race; if fewer than 4 real players are in a
  race, AI-controlled racers fill the remaining slots (AI racers
  cannot win rewards)
- Multiple race formats exist: a free entry-level format, and several
  paid formats with increasing entry cost and payout
- Before each race, players have a 10-second window to secretly
  commit in-game currency toward improving their starting position on
  the grid; all commitments are revealed simultaneously and the
  highest gets the best starting spot
- During the race, players can trigger a small number of real-time
  tactical actions (a speed boost, and a short-range
  interference/disruption move against a rival)
- Spectators can only watch races live — there is no betting,
  wagering, or prediction mechanic of any kind. Please avoid any
  gambling-coded language or imagery in your suggestions (no
  casino/poker/wager framing).

## The racer itself — stats and progression systems any theme needs
## to support

- Each racer has 6 numeric stats (Speed, Acceleration, Stamina,
  Agility, Reflex, Luck) that mathematically determine race
  performance
- Separately, each racer has a 5-tier rarity (roughly: Common →
  Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary), assigned randomly at
  mint/upgrade time. Rarity has ZERO effect on stats or race
  performance — it is purely cosmetic prestige/collectibility.
- Separately again, each racer visually evolves through 4 stages as
  its stats grow through play (roughly: barely-formed → basic →
  developed → maxed-out). This evolution is a visible FORM change.
- Players choose one of 3 specialization paths (speed-focused,
  endurance-focused, luck-focused) that permanently shapes stat
  growth.
- Racers train periodically for small stat gains (takes real-world
  hours, unavailable to race while training)
- Cosmetic/stat-boosting accessories can be purchased and equipped
- A weather/environmental system with several distinct states affects
  race dynamics and is shown to players before a race

## Design requirement: rarity and evolution must be visually
## SEPARABLE

Because rarity (5 tiers) and evolution (4 tiers) are independent, and
we can't hand-illustrate every combination, whatever idea you propose
should ideally let us express:

- Evolution as a change in FORM (bigger/more detailed shape)
- Rarity as a change in SURFACE/MATERIAL (same shape, different
  finish — like a paint job or material tier that's just a color/
  texture swap, not a redraw)

If an idea doesn't naturally support that, say so explicitly.

## Bonus (not mandatory) — a good idea might also explain

- Why "burning" the old racer and "minting" a new upgraded one makes
  simple in-world sense (a repaint? a rebuild? a replacement part?)
- Something on the racer that could visibly dim/brighten as a stand-in
  for its stamina during a race (a light, a glow, a meter built into
  its own body) so players can read its state at a glance

## Track orientation — separate quick question

Should the race track be a VERTICAL layout (portrait phone screen,
camera looking up/forward as racers climb or advance) or a HORIZONTAL
layout (landscape, classic side-view racing)? Give me your honest
take on which fits a mobile-first game better, and whether your
creature ideas change at all depending on the answer.

## Hard technical/platform constraints

- Primary platform is mobile, portrait orientation by default. During
  a race, up to 4 racers are visible on screen simultaneously, each
  rendered at roughly 48-64 pixels tall. They need to stay
  individually recognizable from each other at that size — silhouette
  and color need to do the work, not fine detail.
- Also needs to work on desktop/web at a larger size.
- Runs partly as a mini-app inside a crypto wallet "super-app" and
  partly as a standalone web app — no native app install required.
- Production should be efficient — ideally AI-assisted 2D asset
  generation rather than a large in-house 3D art team.

## What I'd like back

Please propose 8-10 concrete racer concepts. For each one:

1. Concept name (working title only)
2. One or two plain sentences describing what it is — something I
   can picture immediately
3. Why it fits the mechanics above (rarity/evolution separation,
   and if it works, the burn-mint idea and stamina readout)
4. Visual style, described by NAMING 2-3 real, well-known games,
   films, or shows whose ART STYLE (not story) it should resemble —
   not abstract mood words like "warm palette, painterly." I want to
   be able to picture it from the reference alone.
5. One honest risk or watch-out (something that could feel derivative
   or be hard to produce)

Keep them simple and grounded. I'd rather have 8 ideas I can picture
in one second each than 3 elaborate ones I have to read a paragraph
to understand.
```
